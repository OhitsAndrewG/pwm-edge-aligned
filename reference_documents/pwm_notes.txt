PWM - pulse with modulation
 - comes down to one counter and one comparator
    - CNT : timer counter
    - ARR : max CNT then goes back to 0
    - PSC : prescalar
    - CCR : Capture/compare register the number where the output pin flips, sets the duty cycles
    - each channel has a compare register CCR. in PWM mode the output pin flips whnt CNT crosses the CCR. (Duty Cyle)

Definitions:

Clock Sources:
HSE - High Speed Externals - 25MHz Crystal
HSI - High Speed Internal - 16MHz oscillator baked into the silicon, free, walways there - slopy
LSE - Low Speed External - 32.768kHz watch crystal, for the RTC
LSI - Low Speed Internal - 32kHz internal ocillator, inaccurate (watchdog only)
RC - Resistor - Capacitor - marks an oscillator built from on chip R and C, no crustial needed
OSC_IN/OUT - the two physical pins the HSE crystal solder to PH0, PH1 on your board
OSC32_IN/OUT - two pins for the 32.768 kHz crystal
I2S_CKIN - i2s clock in, pin feeding extenal audion book

PLL Block:
PLL - Phase locked loop - circuit that multplies a slow accurate clock into a fast one
VCO - voltage controlled oscillator - the fast oscillator inside the PLL, feedback steers its voltage until it locks
/M - divides the input before the PLL, to land in the PLLs required input window
xN - multplies up to the VCO frequency, The actual speed up
/P - Divides the CO down to make SYSCLK
/Q - divides the VCO down to make 48MHz for USB
/R - divides the VCO for I2S audio
PLLCLK - PLL Clock - teh /P output, the PLLs contribution to SYSCLK
PLL48CK - PPL 48MHz Clock - the /Q output
PLLI2S - A secon, independant PLL dedicated to audio, so changing CPI speed doesnt detune your audio
PLLI2SCLK/I2SRC - teh audion clock and the Mux choosing where it comes from

Distribution - Muxes and dividers
SW - Switch - the system clock selector, Picks HSI, HSE, or PLLCLK. this is the final "go fast switch"
SYSCLK - System Clock - the chips master clock, post selection everything below derives from it
    PRESC - prescalar - a divider. divides a clock by an integer
    AHB - advanced high perfomrace bus - the fast internal bus: core , memory, DMA, and GPIO
    HCLK - (AHB)clock - the AHB bus clock
    APB - Advanced peripheral bus - the slow peripheral bus, hung off AHB
    APBx - x is a placeholder for 1 or 2. one drawing covering both busses
    FCLK - free-running clock - clock to the core that keeps running even when HCLK is gated in sleep - lets core wake up
    /8 - ficed divider feeding the cortex system timer systick
    /2 to 31 - divider on the HSE branch to bring 25MHz near 1MHz for teh RTC
    /1 to 5 - divider on teh MCO outputs, so you can slow a clock down enoght to observe it

Consumers and Control
MCO1/2 - MCU clock output - pins the emit an internal clock so tou can scope it. your clock debugging tool
RTC - real time clock - calender/date peripheral 
RTCSEL[1:0] - RTC Select - 2 bits choosing the RTCs source [1:0] = 2 bit field
IWDG - independent watch dog - resets teh chip if tour firmware hands. deliberatly hardwired to LSI so a broken clock config cant disable it
I2S - Inter IC sound - digital audion interface
